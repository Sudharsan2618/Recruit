Ai-powered Interview Preparation

Max AI Coach simulates authentic interview scenarios by presenting timed questions through a video meeting. It offers immediate feedback, including detailed analysis and personalised recommendations tailored to each student's performance. Additionally, a recorded transcript of their responses is provided for review, allowing students to interpret feedback within the context of their answers.

A Holistic Evaluation Framework

Conduct assessments for your students at any time and from any location with a diverse array of over 6,000 aptitude tests. These tests are meticulously designed to aid students in preparing for campus placements, job interviews, competitive exams such as CAT, GRE, GMAT, as well as banking and government exams, and technical assessments for prominent companies.

Aptitude tests & Soft-skills.

In today’s competitive job market, aptitude tests are the first filter in most hiring processes. Employers rely on these assessments to gauge problem-solving skills, logical reasoning, and mathematical ability. Performing well can open doors to top companies and career-defining opportunities.

Interview Practice

Ethical & fair - Max AI is available 24/7 to interview students. AI eliminates unconscious bias in its detailed analysis of students’ performance in a real timed interview.
